Privacy, by architecture

🔒 Stays on your device

  • Nickname & age selection
  • Chats, memories, saves, favorites
  • AI keys (secure keystore)
  • Settings & statistics

🌐 Leaves your device only when

  • You download public stories from the Kissa story API
  • You chat via YOUR configured AI provider

No Kissa server exists. No accounts. No analytics SDK. No ads. No tracking.
